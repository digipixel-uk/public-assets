# PixlWrk public assets

Public, non-sensitive brand assets served from **https://assets.pixlwrk.com**.

## Email-safe full lockups

Use the `-light` asset on light email backgrounds and the `-dark` asset on dark email backgrounds. Both are transparent PNGs; the filename describes the background they are designed to sit on, not the image background.

### DigiPixel

The full DigiPixel lockup includes the approved wordmark and tagline **INNOVATION, ONE PIXEL AT A TIME**.

- `/email/digipixel-lockup-light.png` — primary colour transparent lockup for light backgrounds
- `/email/digipixel-lockup-dark.png` — reversed white transparent lockup for dark backgrounds
- `/brands/digipixel/mark-primary-transparent.{png,svg}` — primary standalone mark
- `/brands/digipixel/mark-reversed-transparent.{png,svg}` — reversed standalone mark

### Matt Sidnell

- `/email/matt-sidnell-lockup-light.png` — primary transparent lockup for light backgrounds
- `/email/matt-sidnell-lockup-dark.png` — reversed transparent lockup for dark backgrounds
- `/brands/matt-sidnell/mark-primary-transparent.{png,svg}` — primary standalone mark
- `/brands/matt-sidnell/mark-reversed-transparent.{png,svg}` — reversed standalone mark

### Matt Sidnell + My Simple Mortgage

## Source provenance

- DigiPixel assets are the approved DigiPixel Brand Deliverables v2.3 master exports.
- Matt Sidnell assets are from the canonical organisation brand pack `assets/brand/matt-sidnell/v1.0`.
- Raster email variants are PNG exports of the canonical supplied/vector artwork. The reversed Matt Sidnell transparent variants preserve the exact outlined production geometry and change only the approved foreground colours/background treatment.

Do not place secrets, private client material, or licensed font files in this repository.
